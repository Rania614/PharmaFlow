# Interview Guide (≈15–20 min)

Ask open questions. Whenever possible, follow up with "Can you show me?" Don't suggest solutions.

1. What is the name of the system you use at the counter, and who provides it?
2. Which screens or sections of the system do you use most? Can you show me?
3. When a new delivery arrives, what exactly do you do to get it into stock?
4. How do you know which products are close to expiry? What do you do with them?
5. How do you find out a product is running low? Does the system alert you, or do you check?
6. What sales or profit reports do you look at, and how do you get them?
7. Who works here, and what is each person allowed to do in the system?
8. What do you track outside the system, on paper, Excel, WhatsApp or from memory?
9. What is the most frustrating or time-consuming part of running the pharmacy day to day?
10. If one thing about how you work could be improved, what would make the biggest difference?

**Probes:** "When did that last happen?" · "How long does that take?" · "How often?" · "Can the system export that?"
