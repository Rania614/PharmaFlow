# PharmaFlow — Product Vision (Summary)

> Draft v0.1, pre-discovery. The full document lives in Notion (01 — Product Strategy → Product Vision). Positioning is **not final**.

## Vision statement (hypothesis)

PharmaFlow helps a pharmacy see and manage its operations clearly. It turns data the pharmacy already has into timely, useful insight and reduces manual work that the current system does not cover.

## Context

- One pharmacy, one location.
- An existing POS is in use. It reportedly supports barcode scanning, sales, pricing, availability checks and stock display. Its other capabilities and integration options are unknown.

## Problem space (all hypotheses)

Inventory visibility · expiry and batch tracking · purchasing and suppliers · sales and profit reporting · manual work outside the POS · roles and permissions.

## Target users (provisional)

Owner · Pharmacist · Cashier · Inventory responsible person.

## Principles

1. Evidence before features.
2. Complement, don't replace, the POS.
3. Respect the counter: never slow down a sale.
4. No double data entry.
5. One pharmacy first.
6. Accuracy over volume.
7. Choose technology by need.

## Out of scope (MVP)

POS replacement, cashier/sales rebuild, multi-tenant SaaS, multi-branch, e-commerce, delivery, insurance claims, native mobile apps, AI forecasting.

## Success metrics (no baselines or targets yet)

Time to identify low-stock products · time to prepare inventory reports · inventory record accuracy · time on manual admin tasks · user satisfaction.
